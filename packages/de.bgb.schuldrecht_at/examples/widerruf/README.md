# Widerruf — worked cases

Six registered dossiers over §§ 355 and 356 BGB. The collection keeps the live calculator on one §168.4 policy: the 14-day period starts on the day after receipt and rolls an end falling on a nationwide holiday or weekend to the next working day.

The cases cover the last day, one day late, a weekend end, an ordinary working-day boundary, and the alternative start after later information under § 356 Abs. 3. The package does not extend the canon or model the content of Article 246a EGBGB.
